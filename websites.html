Chia Pass Productions

www.chiapassproductions.com

The goal was simple: Create a website that looks professional while also maintaining the whimsical nature of an indie production company. From the animated title to the scrolling images, the homepage is constantly moving, helping it to feel alive. Personally, I love the behind-the-scenes photos on rotation for the hero images.

A fun javascript thing I did for this one can be found on the team page. There's no real leader or typical business structure so I wrote something to randomize the order in which people were presented.

sillyserver.win

www.sillyserver.win

This is the site I learned how to make a website with. It started as a domain to run a Minecraft server, and grew to host homemade games when I wanted to play mobile games but didn’t want intrusive ads. Over time a couple friends joined me in adding to and maintaining the site.

My favorite part is that the “money” you earn from playing the games is transferrable to between games. If you’re bored of playing cherry clicker you can play the flag matching game to afford the cherry clicker upgrade.

Why the .win TLD? Because it was the cheapest one on the Cloudflare Registrar.

No AI Use

noaiuse.org

One day in college I was frustrated with the use of generative AI (I'm an art student by the way). So I wrote this website to deliver some graphics I made to anyone who wanted their art or project as “made without generative AI.” After this site was published the world completely changed and all generative AI was halted.

The real story here is that of a site that’s smaller than one megabyte (if you don’t count the graphics that the site delivers). I remember the first time I found someone who I’d never met before linking to this site. Even though it was only two people, I still felt proud.
